Findings, ranked by decision impact
  1. Untracked LLM value is larger than the tracked channel itself by 2029 — "untracked / misattributed LLM impressions" tracks above the labelled "LLMs (incl. Google AI)" line every year from 2026. Semrush
  2. Total LLM value compounds while organic search value flattens then declines — the two series cross in 2026 and diverge sharply through 2029. Semrush
  3. Citation share is concentrated, not distributed — Reddit and Quora together take the largest share of domains cited in Google AI Overviews; Reddit alone holds 21.85% of top-10 citation share. Semrush
  4. UGC and forum content outperforms owned content for citation — LinkedIn, YouTube and Wikipedia round out the top five cited domains, ahead of most brand and publisher sites. Semrush
Highest-leverage move Attribution tooling is the gap, not content volume. Untracked LLM impressions already exceed tracked LLM referrals, so GA4 / analytics instrumentation for LLM referral detection outranks a content-volume push on ROI. Not worth doing yet: broad content expansion before attribution is fixed — we would be optimising against numbers we know are undercounted.
