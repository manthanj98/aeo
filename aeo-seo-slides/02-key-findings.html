AI search is displacing organic search as a channel — not adding to it

Three findings from Semrush's 2025–2029 AI search traffic projection. The total addressable visitor pool stays roughly flat across the window, which makes this a reallocation of demand between channels rather than net new demand.

Source
Semrush
AI Search & SEO Traffic Study
FINDING 01
AEO traffic overtakes SEO traffic by 2028
LLM-driven visits — including Google AI Overviews — rise continuously while traditional organic search declines across the full window. The total visitor pool stays roughly flat: this is reallocation, not growth.
AI / LLM search Organic search
FINDING 02
A visitor from AI search is worth more than one from traditional search
Total LLM value crosses above organic search value in 2026 and diverges sharply through 2029. A large share sits in untracked or misattributed impressions — so the reported LLM figure understates the real one.
Total LLM value Untracked Organic
FINDING 03
Off-page sources are prioritised over owned pages
LLMs cite UGC and forum content. Reddit alone holds 21.9% of top-10 citation share, and Reddit plus Quora dominate the domains cited in Google AI Overviews. Owned brand pages are largely absent from the citation set.
