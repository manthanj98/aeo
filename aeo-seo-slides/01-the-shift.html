Search stopped being a list of links. It became the answer.

Google AI Overviews and AI modes now resolve the query on the results page. The click is optional — and leading publishers report traffic drops of more than 50% after those rollouts. Visibility has to be earned inside the answer, not on the page below it.

State of AI Search 2026
01 What actually changed for the user Fewer visits — but a materially better visitor
Before

Traditional search

  • Users click links and find their answer on publisher sites.
  • Steady inbound traffic arrives from search into owned properties.
  • Lower intent — visitors land early in the journey, less informed and further from purchase.
→
Today

AI search

  • AI delivers the answer directly on the results page.
  • Traffic drops of 50%+ reported by leading sites after AI rollouts.
  • Higher intent — fewer visits, but users arrive highly educated and closer to purchase.
02 Five learnings that follow from it The link-based web gives way to a direct-answer model
01

Answer, not a click

Information is consumed in place. Impressions and clicks for external sites erode even where demand is unchanged.

02

Traffic decline needs a plan

Marketing leaders must budget for fewer search visits. Early generative-AI adopters lock in Share of Voice within months.

03

From SEO to GEO

SEO ranks a page. GEO gets brand data and expertise embedded in the generated answer as a trusted, cited source.

04

New visibility metrics

Rankings and clicks lose relevance. Share of Voice, mentions per prompt, daily citations and ROI per prompt take over.

05

Category leaders emerge

The Top Brands Study maps who dominates AI answers across 32 competitive categories — visibility is already concentrating.
