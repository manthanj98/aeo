How we'd know it saved the four hours
Measurement
Stepped-wedge clustered on the CSM — 8 weeks, 4 cohorts, pre-registered mixed-effects model. Weeks 0–2 set the baseline first.
Dataset
60 golden reports (12 clients × 5 periods, frozen snapshots), plus 15 rewritten by 3 senior CSMs for a measured human ceiling.
Success criteria
Faster and no worse — time superiority plus quality non-inferiority, margin derived from inter-human SD. Then ≥ 70% still choose it when it goes optional.
Hours freed→brands served→goals hit sooner→share of voice & citations→retainer renewed
